Compare sites

All 12 Earth sites side by side. Moon analogs are compared with the Moon target, Mars analogs with the Mars target ("not compared" otherwise). Each condition is compared on its own: we don't add scores into one overall ranking, because a site can be a great analog for one test and a poor one for another.

How close does each Moon-analog site get to Malapert Massif's hottest ground?

Each dot is one site's hottest good-quality daily reading in 2025 (Aqua, ~1:30 pm local), one 1 km pixel (MODIS MYD11A1, via NASA AppEEARS). The dashed line is Malapert Massif's ridge top measured by LRO Diviner. The closer a dot is to the line, the higher the score. A 1 km pixel averages the ground inside it: single spots can be hotter (80.8 °C has been measured in the Lut Desert).

Dust (AOD)

Data: Earth: NASA POWER aerosol optical depth at 0.55 µm (all aerosols: dust, smoke, salt), 2001–2025 average, ~1° (~100 km) grid. Mars: LMD Mars dust climatology from orbiter measurements (Montabone et al. 2015), Mars Years 24–36 average, 3° (~180 km) grid, for the full local air column at Arcadia's surface pressure (~830 Pa average, from the Mars Climate Database; not the 610 Pa reference), 9.3 µm dust optical depth × 2.6 for the visible-light equivalent (LMD's conversion). Different wavelengths and grid sizes, so treat the match as approximate.
