How close does each site get to Malapert Massif's hottest ground?

Each dot is one site's hottest 8-day average of clear-sky daytime readings in 2025 (MODIS Aqua, which passes over at about 1:30 pm local time), one 1 km pixel at the site. The dashed line is Malapert Massif's ridge top measured by LRO Diviner. The closer a dot is to the line, the higher the score. Averaging over 8 days lowers the peaks: single MODIS readings of 80.8 °C have been recorded in the Lut Desert.
