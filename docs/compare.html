How close does each site get to Malapert Massif's hottest ground?

Each dot is one site's hottest ground temperature from NASA POWER (the hottest hour in its long-term record, averaged over a ~50 km grid cell). The dashed line is Malapert Massif's ridge top measured by LRO Diviner. The closer a dot is to the line, the higher the score. The grid smooths out local extremes: satellites have measured 80.8 °C on single spots in the Lut Desert.
