Río Tinto (Peña de Hierro), Spain

New find (known to astrobiologists)Analog for: Mars (Meridiani Planum minerals) How well studied: Medium to high (team estimate)

Lat 37.724, lon -6.556

What matches

Acidic (mean pH 2.3), iron-rich river depositing jarosite and iron oxides like those Opportunity found at Meridiani

Where it breaks

Wet Mediterranean climate; vegetation; Earth air

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment.

Key facts

River 92 km long; source at Peña de Hierro, 689 m; NASA's MARTE project simulated a robotic Mars drilling mission here in 2005. Coordinates: Peña del Hierro visitor-center parking (approximate)
