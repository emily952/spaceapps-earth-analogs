Haughton Crater, Devon Island, Canada

Baseline (well known)Analog for: Mars (cold desert crater, permafrost) How well studied: High (team estimate)

Lat 75.383, lon -89.667

What matches

Only known impact crater in a polar desert; permafrost; cold, dry, dusty, high summer UV

Where it breaks

Liquid water in summer; Earth air and gravity

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce Mars' low gravity, its thin atmosphere or its radiation environment.

Key facts

23 km wide; Haughton-Mars Project since 1997
