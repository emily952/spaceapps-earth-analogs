Haughton Crater, Devon Island, Canada

Baseline (well known) · Analog for: Mars (cold desert crater, permafrost)
Lat 75.383, lon -89.667 · How well studied (team estimate): High

What matches

Only known impact crater in a polar desert; permafrost; cold, dry, dusty, high summer UV

Where it breaks

Liquid water in summer; Earth air and gravity

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment.

Suggested mission tasks

Field operations rehearsals; habitat tests (team judgment)

23 km wide; Haughton-Mars Project since 1997
