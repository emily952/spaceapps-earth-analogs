Western Qaidam Basin, China

New find (studied mainly in China)Analog for: Mars (Gale crater and Medusae Fossae yardangs; salt playas) How well studied: Medium (team estimate)

Lat 38.035, lon 91.852

What matches

Highest desert on Earth: cold, dry, high UV; salt playas; yardangs and polygons similar to those on Mars

Where it breaks

Still has air, liquid water and life

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment.

Key facts

Yardang fields cover tens of thousands of sq km (counts range 20,000 to 38,800); evaporation 2,590 mm/yr. Coordinates are a published yardang sample site
