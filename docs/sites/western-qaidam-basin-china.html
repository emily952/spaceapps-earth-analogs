Western Qaidam Basin, China

New find (studied mainly in China) · Analog for: Mars (Gale crater and Medusae Fossae yardangs; salt playas)
Lat 38.035, lon 91.852 · How well studied (team estimate): Medium

What matches

Highest desert on Earth: cold, dry, high UV; salt playas; yardangs and polygons similar to those on Mars

Where it breaks

Still has air, liquid water and life

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment.

Suggested mission tasks

Rover tests; instrument tests on salts and clays; biosignature search (team judgment)

Key facts

Yardang fields cover tens of thousands of sq km (counts range 20,000 to 38,800); evaporation 2,590 mm/yr. Coordinates are a published yardang sample site

Climate and conditions

  • Elevation (m): >2,700
  • Mean annual temp (°C): 3.5 (air; July avg 16, Jan avg -10.7)
  • Precip (mm/yr): <20

Geology and minerals

Lake sediments, salts, chlorite and illite clays, carbonate nodules
