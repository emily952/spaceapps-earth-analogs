Kharga yardangs (Western Desert), Egypt

New findAnalog for: Mars (wind-shaped terrain) How well studied: Low (team estimate)

Lat 25.449, lon 30.54

What matches

Yardangs, crescent dune trains and wind streaks like those seen from Mars orbit; core of the largest hyperarid region on Earth

Where it breaks

Hot, not cold; Earth air pressure

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce Mars' low gravity, its thin atmosphere or its radiation environment.

Key facts

USGS and Smithsonian field studies in 1978 to 1984 compared its landforms with Mars; little since. About 200 km west of the Nile
