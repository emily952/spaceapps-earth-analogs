Kamil Crater, Egypt

New find · Analog for: Moon (small fresh craters)
Lat 22.018, lon 26.088 · How well studied (team estimate): Low

What matches

Rayed ejecta, normally seen only on airless or thin-atmosphere bodies; iron meteorite impactor

Where it breaks

Sandstone target, not lunar regolith; one small crater; Earth air

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment.

Suggested mission tasks

Impact geology, ejecta mapping, sample collection practice (team judgment)

Key facts

45 m wide; under 5,000 years old; found on Google Earth imagery in 2008; 5,178 iron meteorite pieces (about 1.71 tons) recovered
