Atacama Desert (Yungay), Chile

Baseline (well known) · Analog for: Mars (dryness, soils, UV)
Lat -24.066, lon -69.864 · How well studied (team estimate): High

What matches

Driest non-polar desert; highest UV on Earth; perchlorates as on Mars

Where it breaks

Warm; Earth air

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment.

Suggested mission tasks

Life-detection tests; rover tests (team judgment)

Key facts

Mean precipitation mostly below 4 mm/yr in the hyperarid core, 0.15 mm in some spots; studied as a Mars analog since 2003. Coordinates: Yungay (abandoned town), approximate

Climate and conditions

Geology and minerals

Saline, oxidizing soils; perchlorates, nitrates
