Lonar Crater, India

New find · Analog for: Mars and Moon (small craters in basalt)
Lat 19.967, lon 76.517 · How well studied (team estimate): Medium

What matches

One of very few craters formed in basalt; target rock similar to Martian basaltic meteorites; ejecta like Martian fluidized ejecta

Where it breaks

Has a lake and vegetation; monsoon climate; Earth air

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment.

Suggested mission tasks

Impact geology in basalt; sampling; ejecta studies (team judgment)

Key facts

1.88 km wide; about 37,500 years old by one dating method (others differ); hydrothermal clays formed after impact

Climate and conditions

  • no data

Geology and minerals

Deccan Traps basalt; saponite, celadonite, carbonate
