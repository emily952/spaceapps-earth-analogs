Bodélé Depression, Chad

New find (process analog)Analog for: Mars (dust lifting and dust storms) How well studied: Medium (team estimate)

Lat 16.956, lon 17.781

What matches

Largest single dust source on Earth; wind lifts dust from a dry lake bed

Where it breaks

Dust is diatomite from an old lake; Earth air is far denser

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce Mars' low gravity, its thin atmosphere or its radiation environment.

Key facts

Produces roughly half the dust leaving the Sahara; dust lifts on about 65% of days; part of Lake Megachad about 7,000 years ago

Geology and minerals

Diatomite (ancient lake sediment)

Suggested mission tasks

Dust mitigation; dust storm forecasting; wind and abrasion tests (team judgment)
