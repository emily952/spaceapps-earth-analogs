East Antarctic Plateau cold spots

Limit site (shows Earth's gap)Analog for: Moon and Mars (cold) How well studied: Medium (team estimate)

Lat -80.367, lon 77.35

What matches

Coldest surface on Earth, about -98 °C snow surface

Where it breaks

Lunar shadowed craters stay near -233 °C (40 K), far colder; ice sheet, not regolith

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment.

Key facts

About -98 °C seems to be close to the coldest Earth's surface can get; coldest spots in shallow dips near the Dome Argus to Dome Fuji ridge. Coordinates: Dome Argus summit, a reference point near the coldest spots
