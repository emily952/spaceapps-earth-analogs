Lut Desert (Dasht-e Lut), Iran

New findAnalog for: Mars (yardangs); thermal extremes for both How well studied: Medium (team estimate)

Lat 30.605, lon 59.068

What matches

Hottest land surface measured from orbit; huge daily temperature swing; large yardang fields

Where it breaks

Hot extreme, while Mars and the lunar poles are cold; thick air

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment.

Key facts

MODIS recorded 80.8 °C surface (2002 to 2019 study); daily swing up to about 71 °C; dunes up to 470 m tall
