Dallol, Danakil Depression, Ethiopia

New find · Analog for: Mars (salts, acid brines, limits of life)
Lat 14.242, lon 40.3 · How well studied (team estimate): Medium

What matches

Polyextreme hot, hypersaline, hyperacidic brines (pH down to -1.7); some pools have no life despite liquid water

Where it breaks

Far hotter and wetter than Mars; active volcanism

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment.

Suggested mission tasks

Life-detection instrument tests (team judgment)

Key facts

Dallol dome is 48 m below sea level; the surrounding Danakil Depression floor is about 120 m below. Mean annual air temp about 35 °C. Hot-spring fluids reach 108 °C (water, not ground)

Climate and conditions

  • Elevation (m): -48 (Dallol dome)
  • Mean annual temp (°C): 35 (air, mean)
  • Precip (mm/yr): <50 typical (up to 144)

Geology and minerals

Evaporites (halite and other salts), iron minerals
