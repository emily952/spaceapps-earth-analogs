What this is

We compare Earth sites with a Moon target and a Mars target, one condition at a time (slope, temperatures, precipitation, dust). Each condition gets its own score from 0 to 1: 1 - |Earth value - target value| / (largest - smallest value in our dataset). 1 means the same as the target, 0 means as different as anything in our dataset. Scores are never added together. Blank data shows as "no data"; we never estimate missing values.

Limits of any Earth analog: No place on Earth can reproduce lunar or Martian gravity, the Moon's vacuum or Mars' thin atmosphere, or their radiation environment. These scores compare surface conditions only.
